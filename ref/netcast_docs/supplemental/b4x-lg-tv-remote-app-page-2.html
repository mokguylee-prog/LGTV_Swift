hi,
I would be pleased if somebody could test BETA version of my new game.
At the time, a few more functions are active, but would like to know if the layout is correct on different devices.
Please send me suggestions and new ideas!

Actual Beta Version 0.4
Features:
- New Game: choose the name of your penguin!
- The penguins walk and can be dragged on the screen
- Feed the penguin dragging the fish on him
- Collect coins clicking on Ice Cube and Ice Cream
- Click on penguin show his Name, Energy, Happyness and food
- remove dung for collect coins and happiness
- touch and remove Bears and Eskimos
- Show XP (experience), Coin (money), Level and generale status (of all penguins)
- button Moneybox - click for coins info
- Energy button (lightning): show bottom panel with Food menu, Energy menu (partially active), Fun menu (not active), Health menu (not active)
- Shop button: open product list (only this at the time)
- Sound button: music on/off
